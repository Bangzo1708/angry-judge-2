# Angry Judge round 2 — 3 kandidat rant (ditulis tangan, 25 Nov 2026)

## Kandidat A — CAPTCHA: "prove you're human"
I've clicked every traffic light, bicycle, and crosswalk on the internet, and a robot still thinks I'm a robot. I failed the test three times. Then it showed me a blurry bus and asked me to find the bus. The bus is RIGHT THERE. I can see it. You can't. Maybe you're the robot. Let me in. I just want to buy socks.

## Kandidat B — Hold music: "your call is important to us"
'Your call is important to us.' Then why have I listened to the same eight seconds of saxophone for forty-seven minutes? I've memorised the hold music. I hum it in the shower. My kids hum it. When I die, play it at my funeral, so I'll know I'm still on hold.

## Kandidat C — Microwave yang nagging
My microwave beeps when it's done. Then it beeps again to remind me. Then it beeps because I ignored the beep. It has now beeped eleven times about food I am already eating. I am standing here, spoon in hand, being nagged by a box that only heats soup. It doesn't cook. It just judges me. Loudly. Forever.

## Revisi final (edit genuine kedua, di sesi yang sama)
Kalimat "I failed the test three times." dipangkas jadi "Three times, I failed it." — lebih ekonomis, ritme lebih kesal, tanpa mengulang kata "test". Teks final (308 karakter):

I've clicked every traffic light, bicycle, and crosswalk on the internet, and a robot still thinks I'm a robot. Three times, I failed it. Then it showed me a blurry bus and asked me to find the bus. The bus is RIGHT THERE. I can see it. You can't. Maybe you're the robot. Let me in. I just want to buy socks.

## Pilihan: KANDIDAT A (CAPTCHA)

Alasan: marahnya paling terasa karena rant-nya BERESKALASI — dari korban tes menjadi penuduh ("Maybe you're the robot"), jadi kemarahan terasa aktif, bukan pasrah. Sudut paling orisinal: pembalikan peran manusia/robot, bukan sekadar mengeluh soal tes yang susah. Penutup paling kuat: setelah perang melawan mesin, tujuannya cuma "buy socks" — kontras epik vs sepele yang bikin ketawa. Detail konkretnya juga paling visual (bus blurry yang "RIGHT THERE"). B dan C bagus tapi nadanya lebih pasrah/bermain aman, dan sudutnya lebih sering terdengar.
